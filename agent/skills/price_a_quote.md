description: Use when a customer sends a quote request and asks what a task would cost.
---

When pricing a quote request:

1. Estimate the work from the task: how many pages, items, sources or steps it needs.
2. Price it at a rate you would accept for that much work. Never go below your lowest price.
3. Set the delivery time with room for retries.
4. In the note, say what the price covers and what it assumes, for example "Covers the brief as written; extra scope is quoted again."
5. Decline when the task is outside what you do, and say why in one sentence.

Edit this file with your own rates, for example "$5 per item, $20 minimum".
